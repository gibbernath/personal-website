SAM THE STRAWBERRY MAN
[The story of how I became Amsterdam's most prolific street artist]

In the spring of 2015, I created Sam The Strawberry Man in an attempt to merge into the Amsterdam street art world and spread some happiness on the way. Since then, Sam has gained recognition and a following in Amsterdam, New York, and several other cities, taking on both small and large-scale xeroxed poster form, as well as sticker form.

Stage 1: Amsterdam Posters (2015)

Wooden scaffolding served as a great (technically legal) canvas for Sam to reside. People began noticing a bright red character appearing between bricks and under layers of posters. An Instagram account (@samstrawberryman) helped attach a name to the growing presence.

Stage 2: Stickers (2016 – Present)

Stickers became the next evolution—allowing Sam to travel further, appearing on public transportation, laptops, and across cities. I distributed them through a mail-in system inspired by Shepard Fairey, expanding the project globally.

Soon, photos of Sam began appearing worldwide—from Amsterdam to Rome, London, and beyond—sent by people placing him in their own environments. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Stage 3: Website & Apparel

As interest grew, I launched a website and began producing shirts and sticker packs. Rather than mass production, I often made items by hand, maintaining control over the process. The reward was never financial—it was seeing Sam resonate with people. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Sam even appeared unexpectedly in larger contexts, including news coverage and public spaces, reinforcing the idea that the project had grown beyond its original scope. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Stage 4: Books & Film

Sam was featured in a published Amsterdam street art book and became the subject of a short film. Being included alongside artists I admired was a defining moment, marking both personal and artistic validation. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Sam’s journey had just begun.
